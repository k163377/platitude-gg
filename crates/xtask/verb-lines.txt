# The verify-ui lines the gate runs, and what each run's picture promises to show beyond the `*`
# row — read by `cargo xtask gate` and `cargo xtask replay`, checked by `cargo xtask verbs` (every
# gate's always-step). The reasons are in internal-docs/反映前テストの機械化.md §選択の正本.
#
#   <verify-ui line>  <components the run's picture shows that the `*` row does not, or ->
#
# The `*` row is what every registered line's picture met (product components only, the census at
# the app's picture: `WindowCensus.reportAt`); a line's subjects are what its own picture met beyond
# them. Both are promises, held against every run (`verify::contract`): a promised part the picture
# lacks fails the run, a part met that no row promises is advice (`shown:`). The lines are registered
# by hand; the promises are regenerated from one run of every line whenever the harness's walk changes
# (internal-docs/反映前テストの機械化.md §観測).
